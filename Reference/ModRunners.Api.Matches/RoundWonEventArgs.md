## Remarks

The round winner banner shows these points, and the match ends if they bring the winner's score to its points to win ([`PointsToWin`](../ModRunners.Api.Lobbies/ISlot/PointsToWin.md)). As in the game, a winner who is knocked out before the slow motion ends gets no points.

Nothing is sent to the other games, so every game must give the same points, for example by deciding from the eliminations it saw. On the other games, a runner's score is replaced by the one its player's game gives it.

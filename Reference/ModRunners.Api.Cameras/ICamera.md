# ICamera interface

The camera that shows the level: the part of it the screen shows, and how far it is zoomed out. In a match, it frames every runner still in the race around the front runner, and zooms out as they spread. In a Solo run, it follows the runner and looks ahead of its speed. It can shake, and the game keeps its view inside the level.

## Remarks

A zoom is in level units per pixel of a screen 720 pixels high, so it shows the same part of the level at any resolution: on a 16:9 screen, 1,280 × 720 units at a zoom of 1, and 1,728 × 972 at the game's widest, 1.35.

The camera of a match decides its eliminations: a runner left outside its view is out, and each game eliminates its own runners by its own camera. So a change to the camera's zoom, place or shake changes the eliminations on this game.

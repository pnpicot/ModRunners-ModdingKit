# IRunner.UseItem method

Presses the item button for the runner on its next frame, as its player or bot would. The game's rules for a press apply: nothing happens without an item, in the 1.5 seconds after GO, or while the runner tumbles, is frozen, pulled by a golden hook or taunts. The shockwave is released at once, nearly uncharged. Every game plays the use, as for a real press.

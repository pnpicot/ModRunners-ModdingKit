# IGrapple interface

The colors of the runners' grapples, the runners of other players and bots included. [`Colors`](./IGrapple/Colors.md) is the rule for every runner, and a slot's runner can have its own colors as an exception. A change shows from the next shot, and the locked hook's from the next lock.

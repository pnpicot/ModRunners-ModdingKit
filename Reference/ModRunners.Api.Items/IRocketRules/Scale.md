# IRocketRules.Scale property

How big a rocket is when fired, its hitbox and its drawing, as a multiple of the game's: 1 by default, a 10-unit box drawn 45 by 30. Its explosion keeps its reach. A rocket does not test its path between two frames, so a much smaller one passes through more thin things.

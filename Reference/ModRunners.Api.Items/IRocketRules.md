# IRocketRules interface

The rockets, and the triple rockets, which give three uses. Each use fires a rocket up toward the side of the runner ahead. It homes in on the runner most in front of it, turning tighter the longer it flies, and explodes on whatever it hits. The explosion pushes every runner in its reach, its own included, and stuns them. The rockets of the map's launchers are not items and keep the game's values.

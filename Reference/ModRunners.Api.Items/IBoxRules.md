# IBoxRules interface

The box, and the triple box, which gives three uses. Used, a box is dropped behind its runner and falls to the ground, where it stays until something breaks it. A runner touching it breaks it and stumbles, losing most of its speed. Explosions, shockwaves, rockets, fireballs and golden hooks break it too.

# IShockwaveRules interface

The shockwave. Held, it charges for up to 1 second, which sends it further ahead. Released, it travels to its target, then blasts there: a square growing from 400 to 800 units wide pushes every other runner in it away from its center, harder near the center. Each runner is pushed once, keeps half its velocity and loses its hooks. The blast also sets off bombs and breaks boxes.

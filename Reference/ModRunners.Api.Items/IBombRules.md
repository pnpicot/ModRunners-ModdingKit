# IBombRules interface

The bomb. Used, it is dropped behind its runner and falls to the ground, and the item becomes the detonator, whose use sets it off. An explosion, a shockwave or a fireball reaching it sets it off too. Its explosion pushes every runner in its reach away from it, its own included, without stunning them.

# IFireballRules interface

The fireball. Used, it is thrown ahead of its runner, falls, rolls along the ground and slides along walls and ceilings, and explodes on a wall ahead. It stuns every runner it touches and goes on. Its drawing grows as it flies, its hitbox does not.

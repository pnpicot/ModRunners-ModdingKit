# IThrownFireball.Scale property

How big the ball is drawn, as a multiple of the game's full-grown ball. It follows [`StartScale`](../IFireballRules/StartScale.md), [`MaxScale`](../IFireballRules/MaxScale.md) and [`GrowthTime`](../IFireballRules/GrowthTime.md) of its runner until a mod sets it, and then stays as set until the fireball explodes.

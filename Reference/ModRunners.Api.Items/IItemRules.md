## Remarks

[`Rules`](./IItems/Rules.md) is the rule for every runner's items. A runner can have its own values ([`Items`](../ModRunners.Api.Runners/IRunner/Items.md)), and follows the rule for the others. An item follows the values of the runner who used it: a rocket its shooter's, a freeze the ice's shooter's.

Every game simulates every runner's items with its own values, and no value is sent to the other games: an item whose values differ on some games of a match plays differently there.

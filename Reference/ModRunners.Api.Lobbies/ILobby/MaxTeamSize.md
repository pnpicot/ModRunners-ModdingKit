# ILobby.MaxTeamSize property

The most slots a team holds, from 2 to 127, or null for the game's teams: pairs, which a slot makes by picking another on its team button. Null by default.

```csharp
public int? MaxTeamSize { get; set; }
```

## Exceptions

| exception | condition |
| --- | --- |
| ArgumentOutOfRangeException | The value is below 2 or above 127. |

## Remarks

With a maximum, the teams are numbered: the team button steps through the team numbers, skipping full teams, and the lobby's host decides every slot's team, the other games' presses going to it. When the maximum is first set, the lobby's pairs become its first teams. Teams already larger than a new maximum keep their slots.

Numbered teams apply offline and in modded lobbies, where every game holds them, and the game's pairs stay in other online lobbies. Every game of a modded lobby sets the same maximum, as from a lobby option. A lobby's teams last until it closes.

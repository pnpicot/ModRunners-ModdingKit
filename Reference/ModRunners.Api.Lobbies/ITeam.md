## Remarks

Without [`MaxTeamSize`](./ILobby/MaxTeamSize.md), the teams are the game's pairs, which a slot makes by picking another on its team button. With it, each slot picks a team number on its team button, and a team may hold a single slot in the lobby, waiting for its teammates.

In a match, a team's slots share one score, and its runners one boost meter and one count of Boosta Cokes ([`TeamsShareBoost`](./ILobby/TeamsShareBoost.md)). They never tackle each other, aim a golden hook or a homing rocket at each other, or catch each other with a golden hook in flight. The round goes on while one of them is still in, and a win with another of them still in gives 2 points.

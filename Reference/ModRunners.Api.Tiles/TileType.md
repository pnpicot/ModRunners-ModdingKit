# TileType enumeration

What a tile does to runners and hooks. Every tile is solid. The level editor offers 16 tiles, and several of them are drawings of one type, such as the plain, stepped and checkered slopes.

```csharp
public enum TileType
```

## Values

| name | value | description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Empty | `0` | No tile. |
| Block | `1` | A square. |
| SeeThroughBlock | `2` | A square drawn as a checkerboard. Runners and dropped boxes collide with it; hooks, golden hooks, rockets, fireballs and bombs go through it. |
| GrappleCeiling | `3` | A square with a white bottom edge, the only tile a hook holds to. |
| WallJumpLeftFace | `4` | A square whose left face, marked white, lets a runner hang and wall jump. |
| WallJumpRightFace | `5` | A square whose right face, marked white, lets a runner hang and wall jump. |
| FloorSlopeFacingUpLeft | `6` | A 45 degree floor rising to the right: its slope faces up and left. |
| FloorSlopeFacingUpRight | `7` | A 45 degree floor rising to the left: its slope faces up and right. |
| CeilingSlopeFacingDownLeft | `8` | A 45 degree ceiling filling the top right half of its square: its slope faces down and left. |
| CeilingSlopeFacingDownRight | `9` | A 45 degree ceiling filling the top left half of its square: its slope faces down and right. |

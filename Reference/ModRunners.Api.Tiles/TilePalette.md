# TilePalette structure

The two colors a tile is drawn with. The game's tiles are black, their main color, with white marks, their secondary color, on the faces that do something: a grapple ceiling's bottom edge, a wall jump face.

## Remarks

The few grey pixels of the game's drawings, on some edges, get a mix of both colors. The transparent parts stay transparent, and the colors' opacity applies to the rest.

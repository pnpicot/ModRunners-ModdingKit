# ITiles.BorderThickness property

The thickness of the border of every tile type that has one and no thickness of its own, in level units, from 0 to 16: 3, the game's, until a mod changes it. A border marks the face a player can use, drawn in the palette's secondary color: the bottom of a GrappleCeiling, or the dashed face of a WallJumpLeftFace or a WallJumpRightFace, where the thickness is the dashes' width.

# IModTileType interface

A kind of tile a mod adds ([`Define`](./IModTileTypes/Define.md)): a look that a tile of the level can take in place of its own ([`SetModTileType`](../ModRunners.Api.Levels/ILevel/SetModTileType.md)). The tile keeps its [`TileType`](./TileType.md), so its form and what it does to runners and hooks. Anything more its tiles do, such as how runners move on them, is the mod's to write.

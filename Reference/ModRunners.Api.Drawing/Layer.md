# Layer enumeration

Where a drawing goes among what the game draws, from back to front. The layers in the level are in level units, which scroll and zoom with the camera.

```csharp
public enum Layer
```

## Values

| name | value | description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BehindScenery | `0` | In the level, behind its scenery, decorations and tiles, in front of the backdrop. |
| BehindRunners | `1` | In the level, in front of its tiles and objects, behind every runner and trail. |
| BehindLocalRunners | `2` | In the level, in front of the other runners, behind the runners played on this computer and their trails. |
| InFrontOfRunners | `3` | In the level, in front of every runner, trail and item, behind the match's HUD. |
| Overlay | `4` | Over the menus and the match's HUD, under popups and the mouse cursor. In pixels of the game's area, from its top left corner. |
